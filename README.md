## Approach to GRC Assessments

My project work emphasizes a structured, risk-based approach:

1. **Identify:** Establish assessment scope, business context, assets, and relevant threats.
2. **Assess:** Evaluate vulnerabilities, likelihood, impact, and inherent risk.
3. **Evaluate:** Examine existing controls and identify control deficiencies.
4. **Map:** Align relevant controls and security outcomes with recognized frameworks.
5. **Recommend:** Develop practical remediation actions based on risk priorities.
6. **Communicate:** Document findings and explain their potential business impact.
